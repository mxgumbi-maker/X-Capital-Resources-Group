Data & privacy

FAQ

Good questions, honestly answered.

Does Margin actually read the photo with AI, or is it a template?+
A photo or typed scores are sent to Claude to draft the summary, strengths, growth areas, and plan. If that call can't complete, Margin falls back to a local, clearly-labeled placeholder so the app never dead-ends.
Is the PIN real security?+
No — it's a household guard that stops a curious kid from switching back into Parent Mode on the same device. It is not encryption or authentication. Treat it accordingly.
How long is our data kept?+
30 days. Photos, results, and tips older than that are pruned automatically, on-device.
Do I need to download anything from an app store?+
No. Margin is a Progressive Web App — add it to your home screen straight from the browser. Native App Store and Play Store builds are on the roadmap, not required today.
